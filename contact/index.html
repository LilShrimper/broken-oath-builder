Contact & Removal Requests

Get in touch about a problem, stored data or game content.

Last updated 11 November 2026

Report a problem

Open Broken Oath Builder and choose Report a problem in the header or footer. No account is needed. For a bug, describe what you did and what you expected. Attach a build or fight excerpt only if it helps and you are comfortable sharing it.

Discord

You can also find the owner in the official Broken Oath Discord. Search for Lilshrimper or Broken Oath Builder and message the owner there. Do not post saves, full game logs or private information in public channels.

Privacy and removal

Use the report form or contact the owner on Discord to request access to, correction of or deletion of information you submitted. Include the short share link or report reference and explain what you want changed. The owner may need enough information to confirm that the request concerns your submission.

The report form does not collect a reply address automatically. If you need a response, contact the owner on Discord or include a contact method you are comfortable sending privately in the report. Do not include passwords, Steam IDs or full game logs.

Copyright and content

Identify the page and specific content, explain your ownership or authority to act, and describe the change or removal you are requesting. Include a way to contact you if a reply is needed.
