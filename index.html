Broken Oath Damage Sim

First, point the sim at your game

The sim reads skills, items and the passive tree straight from your own copy of Broken Oath, so it always matches your patch. Nothing is uploaded: every file is read in this browser tab.

  1. Click Pick the Data folder.
  2. Go to your game folder, usually C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Broken Oath Playtest (in Steam: right-click the game › Manage › Browse local files).
  3. Open BrokenOath, select the Data folder and confirm. Your browser may ask whether to "upload" the files: that only means this page may read them.

The page remembers the data in this browser. After a game patch, use Update game data at the top.
